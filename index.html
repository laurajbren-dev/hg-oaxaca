Safety First, Wine Second

Oaxaca: stay on lit Centro streets and use hotel-called or licensed taxis for anything farther. Watch your drink and refuse free ones. Skip Txalaparta (reviews report spiked drinks and a door incident) and Cuish after dark.

Puerto Escondido: keep off unlit sand paths and skip beach walks after dark. Walk to La Punta at dusk, then WhatsApp or call Sitio Puerto Escondido, +52 958 103 7717, for a taxi home. Ask the price first and agree the fare before you get in. Budget 150–200 pesos a ride and bring cash.
